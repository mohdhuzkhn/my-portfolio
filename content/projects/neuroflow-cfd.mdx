## What it is

NeuroFlow CFD turns a manual, specialist-only rupture-risk read into a structured, reproducible pipeline: simulate blood flow through the patient's aneurysm geometry, extract hemodynamic features, and score risk.

## Where the depth is

The research paper covers the modeling methodology and its limitations in full; this page is the artifact record — what was built, what broke, and what's next.
