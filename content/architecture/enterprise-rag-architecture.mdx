## The pipeline

Documents are ingested, chunked, and embedded once. At query time, the system retrieves candidate chunks, reranks them, and generates a response grounded in the retrieved context — then checks that the response's citations actually correspond to retrieved content before returning it.

## The failure mode that matters most

Most RAG failures aren't crashes — they're the system confidently answering from the wrong chunk. That's why the citation-check step exists as a distinct stage, not a side effect of generation: it's the one point in the pipeline explicitly designed to catch silent retrieval failure before it reaches a user.
